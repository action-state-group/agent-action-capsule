- Spec: `spec/adapters/erc-8183-v0.md` is a new adapter profile for ERC-8183 Agentic Commerce jobs (AAC#583). The job's `description` starts with the line `<contract_ref> sha256:<contract_digest>`. `deliverable` is the provider capsule's Capsule ID, and the evaluator's `reason` is the Capsule ID of a sealed `evaluation-report/v1` with one case citing that deliverable. `met` maps to `complete` and `not_met` to `reject`. For `not_evaluable` and `out_of_scope` the evaluator makes no call, and the job is refunded after expiry. A verifier reports seven checks as `native_check` results: `is_evaluation`, `reason_binding`, `capsule_verifies`, `contract_binding`, `contract_digest`, `deliverable_cited` and `verdict_call`. ERC-8183 is pinned at `ethereum/ERCs` `a078cab5`. The new `vectors/adapters/erc-8183/` has four passing jobs and fifteen MUST-reject ones.
