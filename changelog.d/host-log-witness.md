### Added

- `spec/host-log-witness-v0.md` (informational): a host that runs many holders' shelves has one checkpoint of its own witnessed per tick, and each holder gets a host chain from its shelf checkpoint to the witnessed host checkpoint. Each member has a fixed slot per tick, the tick's salt and tree depth are committed in the host log's entry, and a receiver reports a host-chain stamp as one through a host log, never as fork detection. `spec/witnessed-checkpoint-v0.md`'s follow-up carries the chain as `host_chain`, in place of `receipts`, and the answer to a follow-up with a chain gains a `host_chain` outcome; the answer to a follow-up with `receipts` is unchanged (evidencebook#1085).
