- Evidence Bundle -03 `composed/v1` carried signature profiles (#676): three opt-in profiles in which a verifier checks the signature of a refusal or an artifact response that a member carries. They are `three-member-refusal/v1` (`sig` over `{issued_at, reason, request_digest}`, the evidence-interop/0 and /1 refusal signing input), `whole-refusal/v1` (`signature` over the refusal without `signature`) and `digest-bound-artifact-response/v1` (`sig` over the five response members plus `type` `evidence-artifact-response/v1`). The members a refusal or response carries decide its profile. Each signature is `verified`, `invalid` or `signature_unverified` (nothing to check), with strict Ed25519. They are registered in `REGISTRY.md` §14 and pinned by `vectors/bundle/composed-signature-profiles/` (48 cases). Python, Go, TypeScript and Rust export one verifier per profile, which a caller passes as the composed refusal or response signature hook. Without a hook, behavior is unchanged. TypeScript `ComposedOptions` and `VerifyBundleOptions` gain the `refusalSignature` and `responseSignature` hooks.
- Go: the public package `ed25519strict` exposes the module's strict Ed25519 rule (`ed25519strict.Verify`), so a verifier outside the module applies the same rule rather than a copy.
