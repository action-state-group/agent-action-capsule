- Evidence Result v0: an optional, display-only claim member `reason` (`spec/evidence-result-v0.md` §4.4, #657), one lowercase token naming why a requirement claim is unavailable or why its grade stopped short of `countersigned`. Four registered tokens (`capsule_signature_did_not_verify`, `capsule_signer_not_trusted`, `counterparty_leg_signature_did_not_verify`, `counterparty_leg_signer_not_trusted`), each bound to the claims it may appear on; an unregistered token is valid. A verifier never reads it to decide anything, and a renderer labels it as the producer's statement. The schema, its Go copy and Go `evidenceresult.Claim.Reason` carry it; two positive and eight negative vectors in `vectors/evidence-result/` pin it.
